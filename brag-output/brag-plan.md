# BECCI Opportunity Intelligence Engine — Launch Video Plan

## 1. Product Summary & Creative Angle
- **What it is:** The BECCI Opportunity Intelligence Engine is an autonomous AI-powered procurement, grant, and employment discovery platform designed for backward classes, MSMEs, and entrepreneurs across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and India.
- **The Angle:** Institutional opportunities worth thousands of crores exist, but small businesses miss them because they are trapped in fragmented government gazettes, unstandardized PDFs, and legacy portals. BECCI autonomous scouts scour the web 24/7, parse complex PDFs with DeepSeek AI, verify every claim through deterministic rules, and deliver actionable matches to business owners.
- **Tone:** `polished` (Punchy, cinematic, clean, authoritative dark-mode tech aesthetic with neon emerald, cyan, and gold accents).
- **Target Duration:** 20.0 seconds (600 frames @ 30fps).
- **Format:** Landscape (1920×1080), 30fps.

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Brand Elements
- **Background:** Deep midnight space `#090D16` with subtle cyan/emerald radial gradients and fine technical grid.
- **Primary Accent:** Cyber Emerald `#10B981` (Growth, funding, verified status).
- **Secondary Accent:** Electric Cyan `#06B6D4` (AI intelligence, API telemetry).
- **Highlight Accent:** Solar Amber `#F59E0B` (High-value tenders, grants).
- **Typography:** Modern grotesque (`Inter`, `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `monospace` for telemetry).
- **Surface Styling:** Ultra-clean glassmorphic cards (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` backdrop-filter blur 16px, 1px border `rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`).

## 4. Audio Synthesis Spec
- Sample rate: 44,100 Hz, 16-bit Stereo PCM.
- Instruments:
  - Sub-bass oscillator (55 Hz sine wave with exponential decay).
  - Electro synth chords (minor 7th arpeggios: Dm7 -> Bbmaj7 -> Fmaj7 -> Cmaj7).
  - Analog-style 808 punch kick + crisp 16th-note hi-hats.
  - Cyber riser sweep (100 Hz to 2400 Hz modulated sine with white noise shimmer).
  - UI interface pop/tick SFX synchronized to card animations.
  - Final cinematic sub-boom impact.
